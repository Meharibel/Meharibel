Mehari Belay Meles, PhD


 Wireless communications researcher working on radio-based positioning, drone localization, signal processing, and resilient navigation in GNSS-denied environments. My work combines measurements, algorithm development, and reproducible MATLAB simulations.

 [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=Sk2KUsYAAAAJ) | [Aalto research profile](https://research.aalto.fi/en/persons/mehari-meles/) | [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0005-6682-7152)

 ## Research Interests

 Wireless communications and positioning; drone localization in GNSS-denied environments; angle-of-arrival estimation; antenna arrays; MUSIC; least-squares estimation; extended Kalman filtering; and MATLAB simulation.
